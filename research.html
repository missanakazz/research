The New Dimension of Football Fandom

Detailed overview of the doctoral research project on global football fan consumption behavior and inclusive dynamics.

The New Dimension of Football Fandom is Inclusive!

Original idea developed by Ana CPR Costa. Implementation must be previously agreed with the author.

A PhD Academic Journey in All Continents with a Deep Dive in Brazil

Started at the German Sport University and continued at Pontifical Catholic University of Rio de Janeiro (PUC-Rio).

  • Applies theories from economics, sociology, and marketing to assess markets in different continents.
  • Uses quantitative and qualitative methodology (surveys, focus groups with fans, and interviews with key football stakeholders).
  • Investigates the consumer profiles of women's and men's football national teams fans (socio-demographics, behaviors, and image).
  • Delivers comparative insights across the assessed markets.

Problems & Motivations

  • Women's Football (WF) and Men's Football (MF) follow different development pathways, reflected in distinct fan base profiles.
  • Academic knowledge about WF is scarce, especially when it comes to fans and the perceived image of the sport.
  • There is untapped potential for social and business transformation in football by fostering inclusive studies.
  • A global and comparative perspective on fans' consumption behaviour pushes this development, preventing WF from being appropriated by a discourse of hegemonic masculinity.

1 Research Project Made of 3 Separate Studies

Study 1

Fans' Perceptions: A Comparative Analysis of Brazilian National Football Teams

RQ1: How do Brazilian football fans perceive their national teams?
RQ2: What are the differences and similarities of these perceptions when applied to the Brazilian women's and men's national teams?

Qualitative

Study 2

Only if Diverse: Segmenting Brazilian Fans to Challenge FIFAWWC Stadium Occupancy

RQ1: What are the distinct psychographic profiles of Brazilian football fans?
RQ2: How do they perceive the image of their WF and MF national teams?
RQ3: How do their level of psychological involvement and perceived image of the teams dictate their disposition to attend the FIFAWWC27?

Quantitative

Study 3

Cross-Cultural Consumption Behaviour Profiles of WF National Team Fans: A Psychographic Segmentation Across Diverse Markets

RQ1: What are the distinct consumption behaviour profiles of WF national team fans across culturally diverse international markets?
RQ2: How do national cultural dimensions explain the cross-national variations and similarities observed in these consumption behaviour profiles?
RQ3: To what extent do specific psychographic and cultural factors predict core consumer actions within women's international football?

Quantitative

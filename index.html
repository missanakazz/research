The New Dimension of Football Fandom

Detailed overview of the doctoral research project on global football fan consumption behavior and inclusive dynamics[cite: 3].

The New Dimension of Football Fandom is Inclusive!

Original idea developed by Ana CPR Costa. Implementation must be previously agreed with the author[cite: 2].

A PhD Academic Journey in All Continents with a Deep Dive in Brazil

Started at the German Sport University and continued at Pontifical Catholic University of Rio de Janeiro (PUC-Rio)[cite: 3].

  • Applies theories from economics, sociology, and marketing to assess markets in different continents[cite: 3].
  • Uses quantitative and qualitative methodology (surveys, focus groups with fans, and interviews with key football stakeholders)[cite: 3].
  • Investigates the consumer profiles of women's and men's football national teams fans (socio-demographics, behaviors, and image)[cite: 3].
  • Delivers comparative insights across the assessed markets[cite: 3].

Problems & Motivations

  • Women's Football (WF) and Men's Football (MF) follow different development pathways, reflected in distinct fan base profiles[cite: 4].
  • Academic knowledge about WF is scarce, especially when it comes to fans and the perceived image of the sport[cite: 4].
  • There is untapped potential for social and business transformation in football by fostering inclusive studies[cite: 4].
  • A global and comparative perspective on fans' consumption behaviour pushes this development, preventing WF from being appropriated by a discourse of hegemonic masculinity[cite: 4].

1 Research Project Made of 3 Separate Studies

Study 1

Fans' connection to the Brazilian football teams (16 interviews with stakeholders, 5 focus groups with fans, 834 fans responded to an online survey)[cite: 5].

Study 2

Global X-ray of the consumption behaviour profile of national teams' football fans via online surveys in different countries[cite: 5].

Study 3

The perceived image of national teams: an international comparison via online surveys in different countries[cite: 5].
